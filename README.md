Regional Order Analysis
KEY FINDINGS
1. Top-performing states: Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra received the highest number of orders.
2. Low-order states: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Punjab, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Karnataka, Bihar, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Nagaland, Goa, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu, and Sikkim had relatively low order volumes.
3. Least orders: Tamil Nadu had the lowest number of orders among all states.

Insights and Recommendations
1. Focus on Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra: Continue to nurture and grow the customer base in these states.
2. Analyze and improve in low-order states: Investigate the reasons behind low order volumes in these states and adjust marketing strategies or product offerings accordingly.
3. Develop strategies for Tamil Nadu: Create targeted marketing campaigns to increase brand awareness and drive sales in Tamil Nadu.
